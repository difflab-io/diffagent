Implemented `parse_csv_line` in `src/lib.rs` as a single-pass state machine (`FieldStart`/`Unquoted`/`Quoted`/`AfterQuote`). It splits on commas, allows quoting only at a field's first character, treats commas inside quotes as literal, collapses `""` to one literal `"`, preserves whitespace, maps empty input to one empty field and a trailing comma to an empty field, and rejects unclosed quotes, quotes in unquoted fields, and any non-comma characters after a closing quote.
