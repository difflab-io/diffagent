Implemented a minimal Rust tic-tac-toe library in `src/lib.rs` with `Player`, `GameState`, `MoveError`, and a 3×3 `Board`. `play(row, col, player)` rejects out-of-bounds and occupied cells without mutating the board; `winner()` scans all rows, columns, and both diagonals; `state()` returns `Win`, `Draw` (only when full with no winner), or `InProgress`. Added 9 unit tests covering legal moves, occupied-cell rejection, out-of-bounds, all row/column/diagonal wins, and draws. `cargo test` passed all 9 tests with no warnings; `Cargo.toml` was not modified.
